# Community conduct

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, acknowledge
mistakes, and make technical disagreement useful. Harassment, discriminatory abuse,
threats, doxxing, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
managed by the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content, limit participation, or remove access. They should explain enforcement
privately when appropriate and handle reports discreetly.

Report concerns to the repository owner through a private channel listed on the
published repository. Do not post another person's private information in a public
report. A dedicated contact and any appeal procedure should be published when the
project establishes its public maintainer team.
